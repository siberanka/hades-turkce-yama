## English

Project owner and Turkish translator: **siberanka**.

This is a **non-commercial, AI-assisted fan project**, created to contribute to the fan community and make the game more accessible to Turkish-speaking players. Translation drafts and supporting tools may be prepared with AI assistance and require contextual, editorial and in-game review. No claim is made that the work was independently reviewed by a professional translation team or approved by the game's creators.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Supergiant Games. Hades, its original writing, characters, code, artwork, music, voices and game assets remain the property of Supergiant Games, LLC and their respective rights holders. This notice describes the project's purpose and provenance; it does not grant rights, establish a legal exemption, or guarantee protection from claims.

The MIT license covers only the original installer, validation tools, tests and documentation that siberanka can license. It does not relicense the game, third-party material or the rights underlying a derivative translation. Original game files, another patch's translations, official screenshots and music are not included in the source repository. In-game copyright notices are preserved.

The patch format requires a local game installation and carries replacements and hashes instead of complete original files. That reduces redistribution of original assets; it does not by itself resolve translation rights. Being free of charge and adding attribution likewise do not substitute for permission.

Supergiant's [website terms](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/terms-of-use/) distinguish the website terms from each game's EULA; section 3.5 addresses specified translation and derivative uses. Their [fan merchandise policy](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/policy-on-fan-merchandise/) concerns merchandise and is not a software-translation license. Existing community mods are not permission for this project. An applicable basis for publicly distributing this translation has not yet been verified, so translation releases remain separate from the licensing of our original tools.

The tools use the Python standard library and Windows .NET Framework; those runtimes are not bundled. A local preview can optionally contain official screenshots. Such a build is marked LOCAL-ONLY and is not cleared for redistribution. No game soundtrack is bundled; the user may select a local audio file.

The [Hyper Delivery modpack](https://github.com/cgullsHadesMods/hades-HyperDeliveryModpack/blob/main/LICENSE) uses MIT for its contributors' software. Our original software follows that licensing model; no code or assets have been copied from that project. Its license does not grant rights to Supergiant's material. The community's [Hades II modding guide](https://sgg-modding.github.io/Hades2ModWiki/docs/creating-mods/first-mod-guide/mod-template) also reports a Supergiant policy restricting use of its intellectual property for generative AI training or grounding. That report is relevant to distribution review and must not be presented as approval of an AI-assisted translation. Our code license and attribution do not override rights-holder policies.

Mevcut topluluk modlarının bulunması bu proje için izin yerine geçmez. Çeviri paketinin herkese açık dağıtımı için uygulanabilir izin veya uygun hukukî dayanak henüz doğrulanmamıştır. Bu nedenle kaynak kodun lisansı ile çeviri paketinin yayın kararı ayrı tutulur.

Kaynak araçları Python standart kütüphanesini ve Windows .NET Framework bileşenlerini kullanır; bu çalışma ortamları veya oyun varlıkları kurulum paketinin içine eklenmez. Proje, Supergiant Games ile bağlantılı değildir ve resmî destek iddiası taşımaz.

Yerel görsel önizleme resmî ekran görüntüleri içerebilir; böyle bir derleme LOCAL-ONLY olarak işaretlenir ve yeniden dağıtım izni doğrulanmış değildir. Hades müziği pakete gömülmez; kullanıcı kendi yerel ses dosyasını seçebilir.
